📚 go-scene-builder documentation

go-scene-builder turns a flat map into a 3D scene you can walk through. You give it a map (what is where), a relief (how high the ground is) and style rules (what things look like), and it writes a .glb file that go-renderer’s viewer opens.

Page What is in it
🏴‍☠️ The quest Build a village step by step, from one house to a walled town, in 6 short levels.
🗺️ The pipeline The four stages, what goes in (map, relief, layers) and out (.glb), running it, and use as a library.
🎨 Style rules Writing rules in cascade: selectors and values, shapes, materials, walls and roofs, houses on slopes, terrain.
🌳 Decoration Dressing the scene: facades, plants, models, biomes, rivers and bridges, tall walls and gates, levels of detail.
🏝️ The example island A small made-up island in examples/: its files, how to build and view it, how the pictures were made.
